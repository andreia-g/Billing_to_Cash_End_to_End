**# KPI Definitions – Billing to Cash**



**This document defines the KPIs used across the Billing-to-Cash dashboard.**

**All metrics are designed for finance leadership review, with clear separation between billing activity, cash realization, aging risk, and disputes.**



**Currency: USD**

**Formatting: Currency values shown with no decimals. Percentages shown with one decimal.**



**---**



**## Executive Overview KPIs**



**### Total Invoiced**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Total billed revenue issued to customers.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Represents gross revenue recognized through invoicing, independent of cash receipt.**



**---**



**### Total Collected**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Total cash received against issued invoices.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Measures realized cash inflow from customers.**



**---**



**### Outstanding AR**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Invoiced revenue not yet collected. Excludes fully paid invoices based on InvoiceStatus.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Represents total receivables exposure subject to collection risk.**



**---**



**### AR 90+**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Outstanding receivables more than 90 days past due.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Indicates severely aged receivables with materially reduced recoverability.**



**---**



**### Collection Rate**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Percentage of invoiced revenue collected.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**High-level indicator of cash realization effectiveness.**



**---**



**### Disputed AR Amount**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Invoiced value currently under open dispute.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Represents receivables explicitly blocked from collection.**



**---**



**## Collections Workbench KPIs**



**### Overdue AR**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Outstanding receivables past the due date.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Identifies receivables requiring active collection efforts.**



**---**



**### % of Overdue AR**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Share of outstanding receivables that are overdue.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Normalizes overdue exposure relative to total receivables.**



**---**



**### % of Overdue AR in 90+**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Share of overdue receivables aged beyond 90 days.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Measures severity of overdue exposure and escalation risk.**



**---**



**### Top 10 Customers % of Overdue AR**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Portion of overdue AR driven by the top 10 customers.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Highlights concentration risk within the receivables portfolio.**



**---**



**## Disputes \& Root Causes KPIs**



**### % AR in Dispute**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Share of invoiced revenue currently disputed.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Measures dispute exposure relative to revenue scale.**



**---**



**### Overdue Disputed AR**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Disputed receivables that are past due.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Identifies disputes actively impacting cash timing.**



**---**



**### Disputed AR 90+**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Disputed receivables aged more than 90 days.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Signals disputes that have become structurally high-risk.**



**---**



**### % of Disputed AR in 90+**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Share of disputed receivables aged beyond 90 days.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Indicates effectiveness of dispute containment before severe aging.**



**---**



**### Dispute Resolution Rate**

**\*\*Definition\*\***

**Ratio of resolved disputes to open disputes. This metric compares resolved disputes to currently open disputes to indicate closure throughput relative to backlog, not total dispute volume.**



**\*\*Business Meaning\*\***

**Measures dispute closure throughput. This metric reflects volume resolution, not resolution speed.**



**---**



**## Excluded Metrics**



**- \*\*Average Days to Resolve Dispute\*\***

**Excluded due to the absence of a dispute close date to avoid misleading conclusions.**
