**# Executive Story – Billing to Cash**



**## Context**



**This analysis evaluates the Billing-to-Cash cycle to understand why invoiced revenue does not consistently convert into timely cash and where receivables risk accumulates.**



**The focus is on structural drivers rather than short-term timing effects.**



**---**



**## Revenue vs Cash Realization**



**Total invoiced revenue materially exceeds cash collected, resulting in a persistent Outstanding AR balance. This gap remains present across periods, indicating that delayed cash is not driven by temporary fluctuations.**



**From a finance perspective, this represents sustained liquidity exposure and increased risk of eventual write-offs.**



**---**



**## Aging Risk Profile**



**A significant portion of Outstanding AR has migrated into the 90+ aging bucket. Receivables at this stage have materially lower recovery likelihood and require heightened attention.**



**The aging pattern indicates that overdue balances are not being resolved early and instead progress into long-aged categories, pointing to systemic collection issues rather than isolated cases.**



**---**



**## Customer Concentration**



**Receivables risk is unevenly distributed across customer segments. Small Business customers contribute a disproportionate share of both Overdue AR and AR 90+, increasing exposure concentration in a higher-risk segment.**



**This suggests that credit and collection outcomes vary materially by customer profile.**



**---**



**## Role of Disputes**



**Disputed AR represents a limited share of total invoiced revenue, indicating that disputes are not the primary driver of delayed cash at an aggregate level.**



**However, disputed balances age significantly worse than non-disputed receivables. The majority of disputed AR is overdue, with a substantial portion residing in the 90+ bucket. As a result, disputes disproportionately contribute to severe aging risk despite their smaller scale.**



**---**



**## Dispute Resolution Dynamics**



**Dispute resolution rates are high, indicating sufficient closure throughput. However, the aging profile of disputed AR suggests that disputes are not being resolved early enough to prevent escalation into high-risk aging buckets.**



**This points to prioritization and timing challenges rather than insufficient dispute-handling capacity.**



**---**



**## Overall Assessment**



**Delayed cash collection is driven primarily by collection effectiveness and aging discipline, not by invoicing volume or dispute prevalence. While disputes amplify aging risk, the core exposure stems from how receivables are managed once they become overdue.**



**The dashboard provides a structured view of these dynamics, enabling finance leadership to monitor scale, severity, and concentration of receivables risk.**
